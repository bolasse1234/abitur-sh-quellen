Interlocutor Frame für die SPRECHPRÜFUNGEN (Stand: 01.08.2026)
verbindlich für die schriftliche Abiturprüfung 2029 im Fach Englisch



                                           INTERLOCUTOR FRAME

 Hello, ... (Use candidate A/B/C’s names). This is my colleague Mr/Mrs ... (Use your colleague’s name).
  He/She is going to take some notes.

WARM-UP (Each student about 1 minute)
 Let’s start with a short warm-up. I’d like you to talk to each other for about 2 minutes (2 candid-
  ates)/ for about 3 minutes (3 candidates). The question is … (Choose one question from the list).
 Books – fiction or non-fiction?                         Jobs – dog sitting or tutoring younger students?
 Communication – phone call or message?                  Movies – romance or horror?
 Food – sweet or savoury?                                Movies – cinema or at home on a tablet?
 Free time – book or film?                               Music – live concert or at home with headphones?
 Hobbies – outdoors or indoors?                          Shopping – online or in-store?
 Holidays – hotel or tent?                               Sports – team games or individual?
 Holidays – sightseeing or beach?                        Weather – summer or winter?

 Thank you.

I MONOLOGUE (Each student: 3-4 minutes)
 Now we come to the monologue.

Candidate A
 I’d like you to talk about your ideas, … (Use candidate A’s name). I’m just going to listen (Give the
  task sheet to candidate A).
 Please take your time and read the tasks carefully. Start as soon as you feel ready. (If candidate A
  does not start) I’d like you to start now, … (candidate A’s name).
 (When candidate A has finished the task) Thank you.

Candidate B / C
 Now, I’d like you to talk about your ideas, … (candidate B/C’s name). I’m just going to listen (Give
  the task sheet to candidate B/C).
 Please take your time and read the tasks carefully. Start as soon as you feel ready. (If candidate
  B/C does not start) I’d like you to start now, … (candidate B/C’s name).
 (When candidate B/C has finished the task) Thank you.
(Should there be a communication breakdown) Please focus on the task. / Please move to another
aspect. / How important is … (topic on the task sheet, e.g. the environment) for you?
(Should the communication come to a sudden end) What else would you like to say? / Is there any-
thing else to mention? / You’ve got … minute(s) left. Is everything said?

II DIALOGUE (Two candidates: 8-10 minutes / Three candidates: 13-15 minutes)
 Now we come to the dialogue. I’d like you to talk to each other again. I’m just going to listen (Give
  the task sheet to the candidates).
 Please take your time and read the tasks carefully. Start as soon as you feel ready. (If candidates
  do not start) I’d like you to start now.
 (When candidates have finished the task) Thank you.
(Should only one candidate speak) Remember to talk to each other.
(Should the communication breakdown) Please move to another aspect.
(Should the communication come to a sudden end) You’ve got … minute(s) left. Is everything said?
